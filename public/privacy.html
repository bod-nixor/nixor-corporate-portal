Institutional Visibility

Users should not expect institutional platform activity to be private from authorised Nixor College/Nixor Corporate administrators, Student Affairs, Board/authorised oversight roles, or relevant technical administrators acting within their duties.

Retention

Users cannot demand deletion of institutional records, audit logs, security logs, workflow records, approval records, submitted documents, uploaded files, or other retained data where Nixor College/Nixor Corporate requires retention for safety, compliance, auditability, investigation, disciplinary, operational, or legal reasons.
